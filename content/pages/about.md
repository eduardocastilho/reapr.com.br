I'm a monitoring analyst on the Monitoring Tools team of an MSSP in Brazil. I keep telemetry pipelines healthy, tune alerts and automate with Python. Moving toward SRE, open to remote roles with teams in Latin America and abroad.

## What this lab is

REAPR Labs is my engineering lab. Every project here is run like production: it states the problem, the approach and, when it exists, the measured result. When something breaks, it gets a blameless post-mortem.

## How the work is done

Everything here is built independently, in environments I set up myself, with public data. Nothing comes from an employer's systems, customers or data. Views are my own.

Numbers on this site are real or they are not there. A case shows a result only after it has been measured.
